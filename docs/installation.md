## Three steps

1. In your EmDash admin open **Registry**, search for **WhatsApp Chat Button**, and click **Install**. It asks for one permission, reading your content, so it can show page titles in the counts.
2. Open **Plugins**, click **Settings** next to WhatsApp Chat Button, and type your WhatsApp number with the country code. Save.
3. Open **WhatsApp Chat** in the left menu, copy the snippet, and paste it into your theme once, just before `</body>` in the base layout. If someone else built your site, send them the snippet.

The button appears on every page right away. Change the message, text, position, or greeting any time in Settings; the site picks it up without touching the theme again.

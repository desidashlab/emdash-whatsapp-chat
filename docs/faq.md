## Do I need WhatsApp Business or an API?

No. The button opens a normal WhatsApp chat with your number, the same as a wa.me link. Any WhatsApp number works, personal or business. No approval, no fees.

## What does the visitor see?

A green WhatsApp button in the bottom corner. Tapping it opens WhatsApp on a phone, or WhatsApp Web on a computer, with your number and the message you set, for example "Hi! I was reading About us on your site and have a question."

## How do I know which page the visitor was on?

Put {title} or {url} in the message setting. {title} becomes the page title and {url} the page link, so the first message tells you exactly where they were.

## Can one page go to a different number?

Yes. Open that post or page in the editor and use the **WhatsApp** box in the sidebar. For example, a product page can go to the sales line while the rest of the site goes to the main number.

## Where do I see how many people tap it?

On the WhatsApp Chat page, in one line: how many times visitors tapped the button this week and in total. Enough to know the button is working and worth keeping. The messages themselves tell you which page each visitor was on, because the message carries the page title.

## Does it track visitors?

No. Only the number of taps is counted. No cookies, nothing personal stored, and the chat itself happens in the visitor's own WhatsApp.

## Can I hide it on some pages?

Yes. Open the page in the editor, find the WhatsApp panel in the sidebar, switch off "Show the WhatsApp button on this page" and save. It stays on everywhere else. Use it for a checkout page or a landing page where you do not want a chat starting.

## Do I need WhatsApp Business or an API?

No. The button opens a normal WhatsApp chat with your number, the same as a wa.me link. Any WhatsApp number works, personal or business. No approval, no fees.

## What does the visitor see?

A green WhatsApp button in the bottom corner. Tapping it opens WhatsApp on a phone, or WhatsApp Web on a computer, with your number and the message you set, for example "Hi! I was reading About us on your site and have a question."

## Can one page go to a different number?

Yes. Open that post or page in the editor and use the **WhatsApp** box in the sidebar. For example, a product page can go to the sales line while the rest of the site goes to the main number.

## Where do I see how many people tap it?

On the Dashboard card: today, last 7 days, all time, and the three pages most chats start from. The WhatsApp Chat page has the full list with a period picker.

## Does it track visitors?

No. Only taps are counted, per page. No cookies, nothing personal stored, and the chat itself happens in the visitor's own WhatsApp.
